A face wasn't covered. What do I do?

Add your own cover over anything that was missed. Detection can miss small, turned, partly hidden or fast-moving faces, so check every photo and video before sharing.

How do I manage or cancel HideMark Pro?

Open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › HideMark.

I reinstalled the app and lost Pro

Tap Restore Purchases in the app using the same Apple Account.

Why does the app ask for Photos access?

Only when you tap Save to Photos ("Add Photos Only"), to save a new copy. It can't read or change your existing photos. You pick what to edit in the system photo picker.

How do I free up space?

Settings › Clear Temporary Media and Drafts.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
