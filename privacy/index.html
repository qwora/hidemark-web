Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026 · Developer: Danish Haiqal · Contact: haiqalsw@gmail.com

HideMark helps you hide faces in photos and videos before you share them. It was built so that your media stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Photos and videos are processed on your iPhone. Face detection, editing and exporting all happen on the device.
  • HideMark does not upload your media. The app has no servers that receive your photos, videos, face positions or edits.
  • No account required. There is no sign-up and no login.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. HideMark contains no advertising or analytics code and does not track you across apps or websites.

What HideMark accesses

WhatWhenWhat happens to it
Photos or videos you pickOnly the items you choose in the system photo pickerRead on your iPhone to detect faces and apply covers. HideMark cannot see the rest of your library.
Permission to add to Photos ("Add Photos Only")Only when you tap Save to PhotosUsed to save a new copy of your edited photo or video. HideMark cannot read or change your existing photos, and your original is not altered.
Pictures you import as custom stickers (HideMark Pro)Only when you add oneStored inside HideMark on your iPhone until you delete it.

Face detection finds the position of faces only. HideMark does not recognise who anyone is, does not compare faces between photos, and does not build any database of faces.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Temporary files prepared for saving or sharing. They are removed automatically and can be cleared at any time in Settings › Clear Temporary Media and Drafts.
  • One video draft (a copy of the video you are editing plus your covers), so you can finish later. It is excluded from device backups and can be deleted from Home or Settings.
  • Your custom stickers (Pro) and simple preferences such as your default cover style.

Exported photos and videos contain your edits flattened into a new file. HideMark does not copy the original's location data into exported photos.

Things outside HideMark's control

  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Apple processes your payment; HideMark receives only confirmation of whether you have an active subscription.
  • Photos you save. Saved copies live in your Photos library and may sync through your own iCloud Photos settings.
  • Things you share. When you use the Share button, the file goes wherever you choose (for example a messaging app), under that service's own privacy policy.
  • Links you open (this policy, Terms, support email, App Store review page).

Children

HideMark does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
